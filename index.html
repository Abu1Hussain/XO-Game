How to play & board controls

White moves first. Tap a piece to see its legal moves, then tap a target, or drag the piece. Dots show moves; rings show captures. A red king is in check. Castle by moving the king two squares; en passant is handled automatically. Choose a piece when a pawn promotes.

Move history lets you review the game without changing it. Flip changes the view. Returning to the game picker pauses clocks. Takebacks and draw offers need the other player's agreement on this device.

Draws for stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition and the 50-move rule are detected automatically. Clocks start after White's first move. This is local two-player chess; no online matchmaking or computer opponent.

Rules powered by chess.js 1.4.0 (BSD-2-Clause). Original interface and piece artwork.
